Ballot measures & judicial retention

Three Florida constitutional amendments are on the November 3 ballot, each needing 60% approval to pass — official ballot language is quoted exactly and kept separate from plain-English explanation. This page also covers judicial retention: the yes/no votes on sitting judges that appear near the end of your ballot and are easy to miss.
