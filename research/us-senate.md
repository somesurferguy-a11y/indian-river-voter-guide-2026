Ron DeSantis, sworn in Jan. 21, 2025.**

### Record in office — FLORIDA ATTORNEY GENERAL (2019–2025)
**[VERIFIED FACT]** Major actions, each documented:
- **Affordable Care Act** — kept Florida in the multistate litigation seeking to invalidate the ACA.
- **Texas v. Pennsylvania (Dec. 2020)** — joined the Trump-backed suit seeking to overturn Joe Biden's 2020 win in four states. The U.S. Supreme Court rejected it for lack of standing.
- **Felon voting rights** — opposed broad restoration of voting rights for people with felony convictions; in 2020 requested an FBI/FDLE investigation after Michael Bloomberg funded payment of court fines and fees for roughly 32,000 Floridians. No charges resulted from that referral.
- **Marijuana ballot measures (2022, 2024)** — argued to the Florida Supreme Court against placing recreational-cannabis initiatives on the ballot, arguing the summaries were misleading about continued federal illegality.
- **Abortion ballot measure (Jan. 2024)** — challenged the Amendment 4 abortion-access initiative before the Florida Supreme Court. The measure reached the ballot and received 57% — short of Florida's 60% threshold.
- **COVID-19 (2021)** — sued the federal government and the CDC over cruise-ship COVID restrictions/vaccination requirements.
- **Immigration** — repeatedly sued the Biden administration over immigration enforcement policy; Florida won a significant federal district court ruling on "catch and release."
- **Opioids** — litigated Florida's multibillion-dollar opioid settlements.
- **Big Tech / minors** — filed suit against Meta (Facebook/Instagram parent) alleging "manipulative" design features that addict minors. Separately, her office defended Florida's social-media law in **Moody v. NetChoice, LLC**, decided by the U.S. Supreme Court in July 2024 (the Court vacated and remanded, faulting the lower courts' facial-challenge analysis).
- **"Weaponization" suits** — filed actions targeting special counsel Jack Smith and the New York Attorney General's office over prosecutions of Donald Trump.
- Created a statewide Human Trafficking Strike Force and a crime-reporting hotline; law-enforcement recruitment programs.

**Books:** *Here, Right Matters: An American Story* (Aug. 2021, NYT bestseller list #2); *The Folly of Realism: How the West Deceived Itself About Russia and Betrayed Ukraine* (Feb. 2025).
**Residence:** Broward County, Florida. Married to Rachel Vindman; one daughter.
