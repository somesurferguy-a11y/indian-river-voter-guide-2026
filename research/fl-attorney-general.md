# Florida Attorney General — 2026 Race

Research compiled 2026-08-25 through 2026-08-26 (one week after the Aug. 18, 2026 primary).

**SUMMARY:** Both the Republican and Democratic primaries for Florida Attorney General were UNCONTESTED — no primary election was actually held for this race because only one candidate in each party qualified for the ballot by the June 12, 2026 qualifying deadline (confirmed directly against the FL Division of Elections' own candidate/campaign-finance records, which list exactly four 2026 AG candidates total: two Republicans and two Democrats, with only one per party actually qualifying). Incumbent Republican Attorney General **James Uthmeier** (elevated by Gov. DeSantis in Jan./Feb. 2025 after Ashley Moody was appointed to the U.S. Senate seat vacated by Marco Rubio) automatically became the GOP nominee after his only announced primary rival, Punta Gorda attorney Steven Leskovich, withdrew before the qualifying deadline (and then cross-endorsed the Democratic nominee). Democrat **José Javier Rodríguez** (former state senator, ex-Assistant Secretary of Labor under Biden) automatically became the Democratic nominee after his would-be primary rival, perennial candidate Jim Lewis, was ruled not legally qualified by the Florida Division of Elections over a paperwork issue. No NPA or write-in candidates were found for this race. The Nov. 3, 2026 general election matchup is therefore **James Uthmeier (R, incumbent) vs. José Javier Rodríguez (D)**. Financially, Uthmeier has a large advantage: $1,754,115.52 in his candidate account plus $9,806,527.85 in his affiliated "Friends of James Uthmeier" PAC (combined ~$11.56M), against Rodríguez's $1,101,843.38 candidate-account-only total (he has no PAC and is enrolled in Florida's public matching-funds program) — figures pulled directly from the FL DOE Campaign Finance Database on 2026-08-26. Substantively, the race centers on Uthmeier's high-profile, DeSantis/Trump-aligned tenure (the "Alligator Alcatraz" immigration detention facility, a federal contempt-of-court finding over immigration-law enforcement, aggressive culture-war-adjacent investigations, and the unresolved Hope Florida Foundation $10 million fund-diversion controversy) versus Rodríguez's "people's lawyer" anti-corruption/consumer-protection campaign, which explicitly argues the AG's office has become "the governor's personal lawyer" rather than an independent watchdog.

## 2. Aug. 18, 2026 primary results — VERIFIED

**No primary was conducted for this race.** Under Florida law, when only one candidate qualifies within a party for an office, that candidate is deemed elected/nominated and the office does not appear on the primary ballot for that party. Because exactly one Republican (Uthmeier) and exactly one Democrat (Rodríguez) qualified, neither the Republican nor Democratic Attorney General primary appeared on the Aug. 18, 2026 ballot. There are no vote totals to report.
 Source: [Ballotpedia, "Florida Attorney General election, 2026"](https://ballotpedia.org/Florida_Attorney_General_election,_2026); [Wikipedia, "2026 Florida Attorney General election"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Florida_Attorney_General_election)
