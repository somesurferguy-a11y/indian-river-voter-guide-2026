## 6. Vero Beach City Council (at-large)

**IMPORTANT CORRECTION TO THE PREMISE:** This race is **NOT** on the August 18, 2026 primary ballot and was **NOT** decided in the primary. It has no primary stage at all. It is a single nonpartisan plurality ("vote for 3") election that will appear on the **November 3, 2026** ballot, concurrent with the county general election. As of this research date (Aug 25, 2026), qualifying for these candidates has closed but **the election has not yet happened** — there are no results to report.

**How many seats are up — [VERIFIED FACT]:** THREE seats, not an unspecified number. Vero Beach's five-member City Council serves staggered two-year terms. The three seats up in 2026 belong to the three council members elected Nov. 5, 2024 (to two-year terms expiring Nov. 2026): **Linda Moore**, **John Carroll**, and **Aaron Vos**. (The other two seats — Mayor John Cotugno and Vice Mayor Taylor Dingle — were just decided in the Nov. 4, 2025 municipal election, to terms expiring Nov. 2027, and are NOT up in 2026.)
- Source: Vero News, "2 on Vero council cite big projects in seeking re-election," May 22, 2024 — https://veronews.com/2024/05/22/2-on-vero-council-cite-big-projects-in-seeking-re-election/ (confirms Moore and Carroll sought second terms in the Nov. 2024 election that also elected Vos)
- Source: Vero News, "Newcomer Vos, incumbents Moore and Carroll win Vero City Council seats," Nov. 5, 2024 — https://veronews.com/2024/11/05/newcomer-vos-incumbents-moore-and-carroll-win-vero-city-council-seats/
- Source: Vero News, "Results in for Vero Beach, Fellsmere city council elections," Nov. 4, 2025 — https://veronews.com/2025/11/04/results-in-for-vero-beach-fellsmere-city-council-elections/ (confirms Cotugno and Dingle won the OTHER two seats to two-year terms in Nov. 2025 — i.e., Vero Beach holds a city council election in November of BOTH even and odd years, staggering roughly 2-3 seats per cycle; this is why the "3 seats in 2026" cohort is distinct from the "2 seats in 2025" cohort)
- Source: City of Vero Beach, "2026 Treasury Report Due Dates" (official candidate-treasurer schedule PDF) — https://www.covb.org/DocumentCenter/View/9163 — confirms qualifying window Aug. 10–14, 2026, and **"November 3, 2026 — ELECTION DAY,"** with **no separate August contest** listed for City Council on this schedule. (Note: the City's own election-information web page is headlined "2026 August Primary Election," which appears to be a reused/generic label for the shared county election-administration cycle rather than a literal claim that City Council is decided in August — the city's own official document schedule contradicts that reading and shows a single November 3 election day.)

**Candidates — [VERIFIED FACT], 8 total, confirmed via the City of Vero Beach's official candidate page** (https://www.covb.org/659/Candidates-For-City-Council-2026, retrieved 2026-08-25):
1. John O'Connor (Indian River County firefighter/paramedic)
2. Tracey Lockwood Zudans (former one-term councilwoman, did not run in 2024, now running again)
3. Aaron Vos (incumbent, seeking re-election)
4. Robert MacCallum
5. Jay Kramer

7. Linda Moore (incumbent, seeking re-election; as of a May 2026 report she said she "hadn't decided yet" whether to run, deciding "at the very last minute")
8. Davis Benedict

Note: John Carroll, one of the three incumbents whose term expires in 2026, is **not** on the candidate list — meaning he appears not to be seeking re-election, leaving his seat open.

Sources for candidate bios/status:

- Vero News, "2 on Vero council cite big projects in seeking re-election," May 22, 2024 — https://veronews.com/2024/05/22/2-on-vero-council-cite-big-projects-in-seeking-re-election/
