## JENNIFER PIPPIN (R) — DOCUMENTED PUBLIC RECORD: the "nurse" question

**REPORTED ALLEGATION (well-documented, not adjudicated).** Uncloseted Media, a nonprofit LGBTQ-focused investigative outlet, published "A Moms for Liberty Leader Claims To Be a Nurse. Is She?" by Hope Pisoni, **Sept. 6, 2025** (updated Sept. 10, 2025).
https://www.unclosetedmedia.com/p/a-moms-for-liberty-leader-claims

What the outlet reported it found:
- Pippin has repeatedly been described as an operating-room / registered nurse, including in USA TODAY, The New York Times, TCPalm and Vero News.
- Uncloseted Media filed a public-records request with the Florida Department of Health. DOH replied it was "unable to find anyone licensed by the department matching the information … provided," and (in a 9/10/2025 update) added "there is no way for a medical license to be made private under any circumstances."
- The outlet reported finding no license in the FL DOH license database, the multistate **NURSYS** database, or the **Competency and Credentialing Institute** (which certifies OR nurses); and that the **Florida Nurses Association** had no membership record.
- A separate complainant shared 2024 emails in which a DOH official said Pippin had been **under investigation since April 1, 2024** for potentially misrepresenting herself as a nurse. The complainant was later told the complaint "lacked … a criminal finding." **No criminal charge or disciplinary action against Pippin has been reported.**
- Reported employment records: a **2023 Sebastian City Council meeting agenda** in which Pippin listed herself as "self employed" with Organize Me!, a home-cleaning service. (City of Sebastian WebLink doc id 266012.)

**PIPPIN'S RESPONSE (must be carried with the allegation):**
- She told Uncloseted Media she was "surprised to see these allegations being raised again — especially considering they were addressed over a year ago."
- She has stated her license is not publicly searchable because a court ordered it "hide[n] from the public for five years" after she filed a 2020 fraud suit against someone who allegedly used her license to file for unemployment. She reaffirmed this account by email.
- She did **not** provide a license number or documentary evidence to the outlet.
- She pointed to a stalking case as containing the answer; the outlet said the case documents were not available online.
- Willa Fuller, executive director of the Florida Nurses Association, told the outlet such a sealing order would be "unusual": "We have fought to make nurses licenses private and have been unable to do so even in at-risk areas."
- Pippin issued a "CEASE AND DESIST LETTER" comment to the Facebook page The Florida Fruit Loop calling statements defamatory — but per the outlet, that notice **did not dispute the nursing-license allegations.**

**HANDLING RULE FOR THE GUIDE:** State it as a *reported, unresolved* question — "a nonprofit investigative outlet reported it could not locate a Florida nursing license for Pippin; Pippin says a court order shields it and DOH says no such shielding exists." Do NOT state as fact that she is or is not a nurse.

## CONTEXT: The Sheriff's budget lawsuit and $3.5M settlement (VERIFIED FACT)
This is the biggest recent county-government fight and a live issue in the race.
- Sheriff **Eric Flowers** sued the Board of County Commissioners over his FY2025-26 budget request; the dispute also went to the **Florida Administration Commission** on appeal.
  - AOL/TCPalm reprint: https://www.aol.com/articles/indian-river-county-sheriffs-office-183525916.html
  - WQCS, 2025-11-19 (partial funding in ongoing dispute): https://www.wqcs.org/wqcs-news/2025-11-19/indian-river-county-sheriff-receives-partial-funding-in-ongoing-budget-dispute
- **April 14, 2026:** Commissioners voted **unanimously** to approve a settlement. County provides the Sheriff's Office an **additional $3.5 million** for FY2026. Flowers dismisses both the lawsuit and the administrative appeal and agrees not to seek further FY2025-26 budget amendments absent an emergency. Settlement also sets a **$4 million increase** for FY2026-27, which Flowers agrees not to appeal if funded at that level.
  - WQCS, 2026-04-14: https://www.wqcs.org/2026-04-14/indian-river-county-reaches-settlement-with-sheriff-eric-flowers-in-budget-lawsuit
  - WPTV: https://www.wptv.com/news/treasure-coast/region-indian-river-county/indian-river-county-settles-budget-dispute-with-sheriff-provides-3-5-million
  - CBS12: https://cbs12.com/news/local/settlement-negotiations-to-begin-in-indian-river-county-sheriffs-lawsuit-against-county-commissioners
- Commissioner **Joe Flescher** (the retiring D2 incumbent, who has endorsed McPartlan) on the settlement: "This way we have a consistent supply for the Sheriff's needs, the men and women who serve, and the public at large for the safety and security of our citizens." (WQCS, 2026-04-14)
- **NOTE:** None of the four D2 candidates was a sitting county commissioner, so **none voted on this settlement.** Any candidate position must be sourced to a forum/questionnaire statement, not a vote.
