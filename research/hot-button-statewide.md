**Sourcing rules (carried over from the rest of the guide):** every claim needs a source URL and publication/access date. If a candidate has said or done nothing findable on a topic, that is stated explicitly as a verified absence ("nothing found"), not left blank and not inferred from party affiliation. This file does not infer any candidate's position from their party alone — every claim below is tied to that candidate's own statement, vote, or documented record.

**Labels used:**
- **[VERIFIED FACT]** — confirmed via an official document, roll-call vote, or a dated news report from a named outlet.
- **[CAMPAIGN STATEMENT]** — the candidate's own words/platform, reported but not independently adjudicated.
- **[REPORTED ALLEGATION]** — a claim made by a named party (opponent, advocacy group, journalist) about the candidate, not independently confirmed.
- **[GAP]** — nothing found on this topic for this candidate in this research pass.
