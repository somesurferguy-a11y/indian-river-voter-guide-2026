# Indian River County Special District Races — 2026
### Nonpartisan Voter Guide research file
**Compiled:** Aug 5, 2026 · **Primary Election:** Tue, Aug 18, 2026 · **General:** Tue, Nov 3, 2026

> **Why these races matter:** Hospital District, Soil & Water Conservation District, and Mosquito Control District
> seats are **NONPARTISAN**. Every registered voter in Indian River County — Republican, Democrat, and
> **No Party Affiliation (NPA)** — may vote them on Aug 18. Under Florida law a nonpartisan race with only two
