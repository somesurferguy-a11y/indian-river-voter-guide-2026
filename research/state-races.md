### Florida House District 34
- **VERIFIED FACT** — Florida House District 34 under the 2022–2032 legislative map (plan H000H8013) consists of **part of southern Brevard County and all of Indian River County**. Source: Wikipedia, "Florida's 34th House of Representatives district," accessed Aug 5, 2026 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida%27s_34th_House_of_Representatives_district
- **VERIFIED FACT** — The Indian River County Supervisor of Elections maps page lists exactly **one** State House district map ("State House District 34 Map") and **one** State Senate district map ("State Senate District 29 Map") for the county. Accessed Aug 5, 2026 — https://voteindianriver.gov/election_information/maps/index.php
  - *Interpretation:* This is consistent with Indian River County being **wholly** inside House District 34 and **wholly** inside Senate District 29 — i.e., the county is **not** split among multiple state legislative districts. It is House District 34 that is split (it takes in all of Indian River plus a piece of south Brevard), not Indian River County.
- Official statewide House map PDF (plan H000H8013): https://www.flsenate.gov/PublishedContent/Session/Redistricting/Plans/H000H8013/Maps/30x40_Statewide_Map_H000H8013.pdf
- **Practical note for voters:** because HD 34 crosses the county line, some HD 34 voters live in south Brevard (Micco / Barefoot Bay / Grant-Valkaria area). Brevard voters in HD 34 vote in this race too.

### Florida Senate District 29 — NOT on the 2026 ballot
- **VERIFIED FACT** — Senate District 29 consists of Glades, Highlands, Indian River, Okeechobee counties and part of St. Lucie County. Source: Wikipedia, "Florida's 29th Senate district," accessed Aug 5, 2026 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida%27s_29th_Senate_district
- **VERIFIED FACT** — Sen. Erin Grall (R) won reelection Nov 5, 2024; her current term ends **Nov 7, 2028**. She is therefore not up in 2026. Source: Ballotpedia, "Erin Grall," accessed Aug 5, 2026 — https://ballotpedia.org/Erin_Grall
- **VERIFIED FACT** — Only 20 of 40 Florida Senate seats are on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot. Source: Wikipedia, "2026 Florida Senate election," accessed Aug 5, 2026 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Florida_Senate_election
- **VERIFIED FACT (independent confirmation)** — The official Florida Division of Elections 2026 state legislative candidate extract contains State Senate candidates in **only the 20 even-numbered districts** (2, 4, 6 … 40). **There are zero candidates filed for Senate District 29**, because odd-numbered districts are not up until 2028. Downloaded from https://dos.elections.myflorida.com/candidates/downloadcanlist.asp on Aug 5, 2026.
- **So: Indian River County voters will see NO Florida Senate race on either the Aug 18 or Nov 3, 2026 ballot.**

### U.S. House — Indian River County moved from FL-8 to FL-9
- **VERIFIED FACT** — The Indian River County Supervisor of Elections homepage carries the notice: "Indian River County's new Congressional District is District 9." Accessed Aug 5, 2026 — https://voteindianriver.gov/
- **VERIFIED FACT** — Gov. Ron DeSantis signed a new Florida congressional map into law in May 2026. Under it, District 9 stretches from the Orlando area (parts of Orange, Polk, Osceola) south through Indian River, Okeechobee, Highlands and Glades counties. Indian River County had previously been in District 8 with Brevard. Sources: WUSF/WUWF/WGCU/WFSU, "Breaking down the changes in Florida's new congressional map," May 5–6, 2026 — https://www.wusf.org/politics-issues/2026-05-06/breakdown-changes-for-florida-new-congressional-map ; https://www.wgcu.org/2026-05-05/breaking-down-the-changes-in-floridas-new-congressional-districts
- **NOTE / CAUTION:** The SOE *maps* page still references Florida's 8th congressional district. The homepage notice (District 9) is the newer statement. Voters should confirm on their own sample ballot.
- **VERIFIED FACT — FL-9 candidates**, per the official Florida Division of Elections 2026 federal candidate extract (downloaded Aug 5, 2026):
  - **Democratic primary:** Darren Soto (D-Kissimmee), incumbent — Qualified, no filed Democratic opponent.
  - **Republican primary (Aug 18, 2026):** Ben Butler (Lorida, Highlands Co.); Marcus Carter (Kissimmee); Thomas E. Chalifoux Jr. (Orlando); **Dan Green (Vero Beach — Indian River County)**; Jorge Martinez (Orlando); Steve Rance (Ocoee); Justin Story (St. Cloud) — all Qualified. Jorge Antonio Malavet (Orlando) **withdrew**; Ingrid Nair Morales Vazquez (Kissimmee) **did not qualify**.
  - *Note for local readers:* Dan Green is the only FL-9 congressional candidate whose address of record is in Indian River County.
