> Standard applied: a filed lawsuit is an **accusation**, not a finding. Outcomes are given wherever obtained.

### 1. Teel v. Lozada / Sheriff of Indian River County — **RESOLVED IN THE SHERIFF'S FAVOR**
- **[VF] Facts:** On **July 26, 2017**, Susan Teel, 62, attempted suicide at her Vero Beach home. Deputy **Jonathan Lozada** responded to a 911 call placed by her daughter, intending a Baker Act custody (Fla. Stat. § 394.463). Lozada shot and killed Mrs. Teel.
- **[VF]** A **grand jury declined to indict** Lozada (January 2018).
- **[RA → resolved]** September 2018: Dr. **Dudley Teel**, as personal representative of the estate, filed a **$10 million federal suit** naming **Sheriff Deryl Loar** and Deputy Lozada — two claims under **42 U.S.C. § 1983**: excessive force (Fourth Amendment) and a *Monell* claim against the Sheriff's Office. (Two Florida wrongful-death claims were later **voluntarily dismissed** by the estate.)
  Source: Vero News 9/10/2018 — http://veronews.com/2018/09/10/teel-attorney-files-10-million-wrongful-death-lawsuit-sheriffs-office/ ; WPTV — https://www.wptv.com/news/region-martin-county/stuart/family-of-woman-shot-by-indian-river-county-sheriffs-deputy-files-wrongful-death-lawsuit
- **[VF] Procedural history:** District court (S.D. Fla., **No. 2:18-cv-14367-DMM**) granted summary judgment for both defendants; the **Eleventh Circuit reversed in part and vacated in part** — *Teel v. Lozada*, **826 F. App'x 880 (11th Cir. 2020)** ("Teel I"). On remand the district court granted summary judgment on the ***Monell*** claim, and the **excessive-force claim went to a jury trial (March 2022)**.
- **[VF] FINAL OUTCOME: the jury found Deputy Lozada did NOT use excessive force.** The Eleventh Circuit **AFFIRMED as to all issues** — *Teel v. Lozada*, **No. 22-11106**, opinion filed **April 18, 2024** (Lagoa, J.; before Jordan, Lagoa, Marcus).
  Primary source (published opinion PDF): https://media.ca11.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/files/202211106.pdf
- **[VF] Note on the caption:** by the appeal, the official-capacity defendant was **Sheriff Eric Flowers**, automatically substituted for Loar upon Loar leaving office (Fed. R. Civ. P. 25(d)). The conduct at issue occurred under **Loar's** tenure.
- **Fair characterization for the guide:** *The most serious civil case naming Loar in his official capacity as sheriff was litigated to a jury and to a federal appellate affirmance, and the Sheriff's Office prevailed on every claim. No liability was found. No money was paid on this claim.*

### 2. U.S. DOJ Office for Civil Rights investigation — **VERIFIED FEDERAL INVESTIGATION, NO VIOLATION FOUND**
- **[VF]** **February 4, 2019** — the **Office for Civil Rights (OCR), Office of Justice Programs, U.S. Department of Justice** issued a **"Notice of Findings"** letter addressed to **Sheriff Deryl Loar, Indian River Sheriff's Office, 4055 41st Avenue, Vero Beach, FL 32960**, in *Complaint No. **17-OCR-1042***.
  Primary source: https://www.ojp.gov/sites/g/files/xyckuh241/files/media/document/no17ocr1042nof_redacted.pdf
- **[VF] Allegation:** the complainant alleged IRCSO **unlawfully discriminated against him on the basis of disability by failing to provide effective communication** in connection with his arrest and detention.
- **[VF] Jurisdiction:** IRCSO was subject to OCR enforcement because it receives DOJ/OJP federal financial assistance — named grants **BJA 2015-BU-BX-15076445** (8/11/2015–8/11/2020) and **BJA 2018-BO-BX-18095304** (9/13/2018–9/13/2023).
- **[VF] FINDING:** OCR "carefully reviewed the evidence" and **concluded the evidence does NOT support a finding that Respondent is in violation** of **Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act** (29 U.S.C. § 794) and/or **Title II of the ADA**.
- **[VF] BUT — the material caveat:** OCR wrote that evidence obtained during the investigation **"raised concerns regarding the IRCSO's policies, procedures, and trainings for effective communication with individuals who have communication disabilities,"** and that **IRCSO "has proactively adopted several changes to ameliorate these concerns."**
- **[VF]** OCR footnoted that the complainant was arrested by federal authorities on unrelated criminal charges during the investigation, pled guilty, and remained in federal custody.
- **Fair characterization:** *A federal civil-rights investigation of Loar's agency closed with no violation found, but DOJ documented concerns about the agency's disability-communication policies and training, which the agency changed.* Both halves must be reported together.

### The November 2026 property tax amendment (Amendment 3)
- **[VF]** **Amendment 3 (CS/HJR 1F)** is on the **November 3, 2026** ballot; requires **60%** to pass. It would replace the current homestead exemption structure with a **$25,000 exemption against school millages**, up to **$150,000 against non-school millages beginning Jan. 1, 2027**, and up to **$250,000 beginning Jan. 1, 2028**. It would also cut the assessment-increase cap on **non-homestead** property from **10% to 5%**.
- **[VF] Indian River County fiscal impact:** county projections estimate **$22 million** in lost property-tax revenue in the first year and another **$19 million** the following year. The **Indian River County Taxpayers Association** projects roughly **$50 million** in lost ad valorem revenue once fully phased in.
- **[VF]** County Administrator **John Titkanich** warned of cuts to public safety, infrastructure, recreation, citizen/children's services, libraries and veteran services. **Vice Chair Laura Moss:** *"They've declared war on local government."* **Christen Brewer, President of Indian River Firefighters:** *"About 79.1% of our funding does come from ad valorem taxes."*
  Sources: WFLX 6/12/2026 — https://www.wflx.com/2026/06/12/indian-river-county-leaders-warn-property-tax-amendment-could-cost-county-50m/ ; Sebastian Daily — https://www.sebastiandaily.com/business/indian-river-county-leaders-warn-proposed-property-tax-amendment-could-cost-county-93243/ ; FY26-27 budget workshop book, July 8, 2026 — https://www.indianriver.gov/Document%20Center/Services/Management%20&%20Budget/Annual%20Budget%20Documents/2026-27/Budget-Workshop-Book-2026-27.pdf
