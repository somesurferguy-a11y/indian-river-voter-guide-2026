## ⚠️ CRITICAL CORRECTION TO THE RESEARCH PREMISE

The tasking described "the November property tax amendment (HJR 203 … House passed it 75-26 around June 2, 2026)." That conflates **two different measures**. Both are documented here because the distinction changes what Brackett's record actually is.

**1. CS/CS/HJR 203 (2026 Regular Session) — "Elimination of Non-school Property Taxes for Homesteads"**
- Would have phased out ALL non-school homestead ad valorem taxes by raising the exemption $100,000/yr for 10 years beginning 2027, full elimination 2037. Also barred counties/cities from cutting law-enforcement funding below 2024/2025 levels.
- **House passed it 80–30 on 2/19/2026** (Third Reading, 11:23 AM, Session Day 38).
- **DIED in Senate Appropriations 3/13/2026.** It is NOT on the November ballot.
- Source: https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2026/203 (accessed 8/5/2026); roll call PDF https://flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2026/203/Vote/HouseVote_h00203c2523.PDF
- **VERIFIED FACT — Brackett did NOT vote.** The official roll call lists "Brackett-34" with no Y or N marking; he is one of the 7 members recorded **Not Voting**. (Roll call PDF, 2/19/2026, read directly.)

**2. CS/HJR 1-F (2026F Special Session, June 1–3, 2026) — "Save Our Homes from Excessive Property Taxes"** — *this is the measure actually on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot.*
- Raises the homestead exemption for non-school levies from $50,000 to **$150,000 in 2027** and **$250,000 in 2028**, and directs the Legislature to set a schedule for full elimination of non-school homestead property taxes. Effective Jan 1, 2027 if approved. Needs 60% voter approval.
- **House passed 75–26 on 6/2/2026** (Third Reading, 12:29 PM, Session Day 2). Senate passed 30–9 same day. Signed by officers / filed with Secretary of State 6/16/2026.
- Source: https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2026F/1F (accessed 8/5/2026); House roll call PDF https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2026F/1F/Vote/HouseVote_h00001Fc1900.PDF
- **VERIFIED FACT — Brackett voted YEA.** Roll call shows "Y Brackett-34." (Roll call PDF, 6/2/2026, read directly.)
- Four Democratic floor amendments failed the same day (25–74, 25–71, 26–75, 25–75).

## 5. DOCUMENTED PUBLIC RECORD (opposition research)

### A. Ohio dental board discipline — 1990s case
**VERIFIED FACT (primary source: published appellate opinion).** *Sohi v. Ohio State Dental Board*, Court of Appeals of Ohio, First District, Hamilton County, **No. C-970739, decided October 23, 1998** (https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/oh-court-of-appeals/1471110.html).
- The Ohio State Dental Board found that between **November 1994 and March 1995** Sohi "departed from the accepted standards of the profession by mistreating and abusing his young patients," in violation of R.C. 4715.30(A)(7). Board findings recited in the opinion include applying hand-over-mouth techniques to deliberately prevent a child from breathing, grabbing patients by the throat and placing them in headlocks, holding a patient against the operatory wall and shaking him, and threatening children with needles.
- **Sanction:** six-month license suspension; reinstatement conditioned on mental-health evaluation and treatment; five-year probation; staff supervision during patient treatment.
- **Outcome on appeal — IMPORTANT NUANCE:** the court **affirmed in part and reversed in part**.
