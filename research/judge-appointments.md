# Judicial Appointments Research — Indian River County Voter Guide 2026

This file documents, for each judge appearing on the 2026 ballot (merit retention on Nov 3, or unopposed in the Aug 18 19th Circuit primary), which Florida governor appointed them to their CURRENT seat and in what year — or confirms that they won an original election to that seat instead of being appointed. Research completed via Ballotpedia, official 4th DCA / 19th Circuit / Governor's office pages, The Florida Bar News, and news coverage.

## Detail Notes

### Florida Supreme Court

**Carlos G. Muñiz** — Appointed by Gov. Ron DeSantis, took office Jan. 22, 2019. Already documented in the guide; not re-researched in depth per instructions, but this matches independent knowledge and is consistent with all other sourcing conventions used below.

### Fourth District Court of Appeal (merit retention, Nov 3, 2026)

**1. Alan O. Forst** — Appointed by Gov. Rick Scott (R) to the Fourth District Court of Appeal on March 7, 2013; took the oath of office April 8, 2013. Retained by voters in 2014 (75.1%) and 2020 (64.1%).
Source: [Alan O. Forst - Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Alan_O._Forst) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by [Judge Alan O. Forst — 4th DCA](https://4dca.flcourts.gov/judges/judge-alan-o.-forst).

**2. Mark W. Klingensmith** — Appointed by Gov. Rick Scott (R) to the Fourth District Court of Appeal on June 27, 2013. Retained by voters in 2014 and again Nov. 3, 2020 (63.0%). Later served as Chief Judge of the 4th DCA.
Source: [Mark W. Klingensmith - Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Mark_W._Klingensmith) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by [4th DCA Court News — Klingensmith Re-elected Chief Judge](https://4dca.flcourts.gov/About-the-Court/Court-News/Judge-Mark-W.-Klingensmith-Re-elected-as-Fourth-DCA-Chief-Judge-and-Judge-Jeffrey-T.-Kuntz-Named-Chief-Judge-elect).

**3. Johnathan D. Lott** (also styled "Jonathan D. Lott" in some sources) — Appointed by Gov. Ron DeSantis to the Fourth DCA on Nov. 3, 2025; assumed office Nov. 12, 2025. Previously a 17th Judicial Circuit (Broward) judge, himself appointed to that circuit court seat by DeSantis in November 2024 (took the bench Jan. 1, 2025). A U.Chicago Law 2014 graduate and former Assistant U.S. Attorney (Civil Division).
Source: [Judge Jonathan D. Lott appointed to the Fourth DCA – The Florida Bar](https://www.floridabar.org/the-florida-bar-news/judge-jonathan-d-lott-appointed-to-the-fourth-dca/) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by [Governor appoints Jonathan Lott to 4th DCA — Florida Politics](https://floridapolitics.com/archives/763399-gov-desantis-appoints-jonathan-lott-to-4th-district-court-of-appeal/) and [17th Judicial Circuit announcement PDF](https://www.17th.flcourts.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Governor-Appoints-Jonathan-D.-Lott-to-Fourth-DCA.pdf).

**4. Shannon K. Shaw** — Appointed by Gov. Ron DeSantis to the Fourth DCA on July 17, 2025. Of Jupiter, FL; was an Assistant U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of Florida since 2017; Duke University (B.A.) and Vanderbilt Law (J.D.).
Source: [Governor Ron DeSantis Makes Four Judicial Appointments — Executive Office of the Governor](https://www.flgov.com/eog/news/press/2025/governor-ron-desantis-makes-four-judicial-appointments-0) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by [Shannon K. Shaw - Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Shannon_K._Shaw) and [Governor appoints nine judges in July – The Florida Bar](https://www.floridabar.org/the-florida-bar-news/governor-appoints-nine-judges-in-july/).

**5. Caroline Cahill Shepherd** — Appointed by Gov. Ron DeSantis to the Fourth DCA on Oct. 1, 2025, filling the vacancy from Judge Sheree Cunningham's elevation/retirement. Previously a 15th Judicial Circuit (Palm Beach) circuit judge since 2019 and before that a Palm Beach County Court judge and Assistant State Attorney.
Source: [Vacancy caused by the Retirement of Judge Sheree Cunningham and the Elevation of Judge Caroline Shepherd — Executive Office of the Governor](https://www.flgov.com/eog/node/5160) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by [Caroline Shepherd - Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Caroline_Shepherd) and [Judge Caroline Shepherd — 4th DCA](https://4dca.flcourts.gov/judges/judge-caroline-shepherd).

### 19th Judicial Circuit (unopposed, Aug 18, 2026 primary)

**6. Michael C. Heisey** — NOT appointed. Won a contested original election in 2014 (53.62% of the vote against opponent Steven Ziskinder) for a six-year term beginning Jan. 6, 2015. Prior to running, he was a sole practitioner (Law Office of Michael C. Heisey, 2005–2014) with eight years in the Army Reserve.
Source: [Michael C. Heisey - Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Michael_C._Heisey) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by [Judge Michael C. Heisey — Circuit19](https://www.circuit19.org/judges/judge-michael-c-heisey/).

**7. Michael Linn** (also seen as "Michael J. Linn") — Appointed by Gov. Rick Scott to the 19th Judicial Circuit in July 2018, filling the vacancy created by the retirement of Judge Paul B. Kanarek; did not take the bench until Kanarek's retirement took effect (reported as starting in January). Previously an Assistant State Attorney on the Treasure Coast (2005–2018), including five years as County Court Supervisor in Martin County.
Source: [Judge Michael J. Linn — Circuit19](https://www.circuit19.org/judges/judge-michael-j-linn/) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by news coverage in [New judge assigned to Palm Bay corruption cases — ClickOrlando](https://www.clickorlando.com/news/2019/07/12/new-judge-assigned-to-palm-bay-corruption-cases/) and [Judges appointed by Rick Scott - Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Judges_appointed_by_Rick_Scott). Note: exact appointment date (month/day) not independently confirmed beyond "July 2018" — flagged as approximate.

**8. Steven J. Levin** — Appointed by Gov. Jeb Bush to the 19th Judicial Circuit in 1999. Later elected Chief Judge of the 19th Circuit in 2009, serving until 2015.
Source: [Steven J. Levin - Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Steven_J._Levin) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by [Judge Steven J. Levin — Circuit19](https://www.circuit19.org/judges/judge-steven-j-levin/). Note: exact month/day of the 1999 appointment not independently confirmed — flagged as approximate (year only).

**9. Michael David Porter** — Appointed by Gov. Ron DeSantis to the 19th Judicial Circuit on July 23, 2025, succeeding a judge who vacated the seat. Of Vero Beach; an Assistant U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of Florida since 2018 (previously in the Eastern and Western Districts of Tennessee). Currently assigned to civil jury trials in St. Lucie County.
Source: [Governor makes a 19th Circuit judicial appointment – The Florida Bar](https://www.floridabar.org/the-florida-bar-news/governor-makes-a-19th-circuit-judicial-appointment-3/) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by [Governor DeSantis appoints Michael Porter to Nineteenth Judicial Circuit Court — WQCS](https://www.wqcs.org/wqcs-news/2025-07-23/governor-desantis-appoints-michael-porter-to-nineteenth-judicial-circuit-court) and [Gov. DeSantis taps prosecutor Michael Porter — Florida Politics](https://floridapolitics.com/archives/748456-gov-desantis-taps-prosecutor-michael-porter-to-succeed-judge-whose-son-robbed-a-smoke-shop/).

**10. Laurie (Ehler) Buchanan** — NOT appointed. Ran for an open seat in the 2014 general election and won unopposed (automatically elected without opposition on the ballot), for a six-year term beginning Jan. 5, 2015. Previously spent eight years as a hearing officer for the 19th Circuit, and before that was a full-time guardian ad litem and attorney in private practice.
Source: [Laurie Ehler Buchanan - Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Laurie_Ehler_Buchanan) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by [Judge Laurie E. Buchanan — Circuit19](https://www.circuit19.org/judges/judge-laurie-e-buchanan/).

**11. Victoria L. Griffin** — NOT appointed. Ran for an open seat (vacancy from the retirement of Hon. Larry Schack) in the Nov. 4, 2014 general election and won unopposed, for a six-year term beginning Jan. 5, 2015.
Source: [Victoria L. Griffin - Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Victoria_L._Griffin) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by [Judge Victoria L. Griffin — Circuit19](https://www.circuit19.org/judges/judge-victoria-l-griffin/).

**12. Lillian B. Ewen** — Appointed by Gov. Ron DeSantis to the 19th Judicial Circuit, announced June 18, 2024. Previously a general magistrate in the 19th Circuit since 2021 and a senior staff attorney for the circuit before that. Granddaughter of the late John M. McCarty, a Fort Pierce attorney/circuit judge who was chief of staff to his brother, Gov. Daniel McCarty.
Source: [University of Florida graduate appointed to serve as judge on 19th Judicial Circuit Court — WCJB](https://www.wcjb.com/2024/06/18/university-florida-graduate-appointed-serve-judge-19th-judicial-circuit-court/) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by [Governor makes five judicial appointments in June – The Florida Bar](https://www.floridabar.org/the-florida-bar-news/governor-makes-five-judicial-appointments-in-june/) and [Circuit Judge Lillian B. Ewen takes oath of office April 25 — Yahoo News / TCPalm](https://www.yahoo.com/news/circuit-judge-lillian-b-ewen-161318901.html).

**13. Cynthia L. Cox** — NOT appointed by a governor, per available sourcing. Elected to the 19th Judicial Circuit on Nov. 5, 1996 (re-elected 2008 and 2014, apparently unopposed in later cycles). Before the bench she practiced law in Indian River County from 1986 (associate, then own firm from 1988).
Source: [Cynthia Cox (Florida) - Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Cynthia_Cox_(Florida)) (accessed 2026-08-26); corroborated by [Judge Cynthia L. Cox — Circuit19](https://www.circuit19.org/judges/judge-cynthia-l-cox/).
**Caveat:** I was not able to confirm from available sources whether Cox's original 1996 race was contested (i.e., whether she had a primary or general-election opponent) or won unopposed like Buchanan and Griffin's 2014 races. What is confirmed is that she reached the bench via election, not gubernatorial appointment. Flagging this distinction explicitly per the no-fabrication policy — the guide should describe her as "won election to the seat in 1996" without claiming it was contested unless that detail is verified separately.

**14. Elizabeth Ann Metzger** — Appointed by Gov. Jeb Bush to the 19th Judicial Circuit on April 10, 2006. Re-elected 2008 and 2014. Practiced law as a partner at Peterson Bernard before her appointment; later served a term as Chief Judge of the 19th Circuit, during which she implemented a courthouse therapy-dog program.
Source: [Elizabeth A. Metzger - Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Elizabeth_A._Metzger) (accessed 2026-08-26).
**Caveat:** A PDF found on the Governor's website is filed under a "4th DCA" filename (`metzger_elizabeth_4th_dca.pdf`), which raised a question about whether she was ever nominated/appointed to the 4th DCA rather than (or in addition to) the 19th Circuit. That PDF could not be retrieved (403 error) to check its contents, and no other source found indicates Metzger currently sits on, or was ever appointed to, the 4th DCA — she is confirmed to be running for retention/re-election to the 19th Circuit trial court in the Aug. 18, 2026 primary per the guide's own list. This is noted for transparency but does not change the finding: her current (19th Circuit) seat traces to the 2006 Jeb Bush appointment. If the guide team wants this loose end fully closed, someone with direct access to flgov.com PDFs (or the Florida JNC archives) could check whether that file is simply an old, unsuccessful 4th DCA application from earlier in her career.

## Sourcing Notes / Limitations

- Primary source used throughout: Ballotpedia judge profile pages, cross-checked against official 4th DCA (4dca.flcourts.gov) and 19th Circuit (circuit19.org) judge bio pages, Florida Bar News appointment announcements, and Governor's Office (flgov.com) press releases where available.
- Direct WebFetch access to Ballotpedia pages was blocked/returned empty in this session (likely bot protection); findings rely on search-engine-indexed snippets of those same Ballotpedia pages plus independent corroborating sources (court websites, Florida Bar News, local news). Each entry above lists at least one corroborating source beyond Ballotpedia where one was found.
- Exact day-level appointment dates were not confirmed for Michael Linn (month only: July 2018) and Steven J. Levin (year only: 1999). Everything else has a specific date.
- No judge's information was fabricated or guessed; where a detail could not be verified (Cox's 1996 race contestedness; the Metzger 4th DCA PDF anomaly) it is flagged explicitly above rather than asserted.
