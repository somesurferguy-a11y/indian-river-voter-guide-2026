# U.S. Senate (FL Special Election) — Candidate Policy Positions, in Their Own Words

**Purpose of this file:** The main research file (`us-senate.md`) documents biography, voting record, finance, and endorsements for the 2026 Florida U.S. Senate special election. It was thin on candidates' own stated **policy positions**, especially for Angie Nixon and Neil Gillespie. This file fills that gap for the three candidates who advanced to the **Nov. 3, 2026 general election**: **Ashley Moody (R, incumbent)**, **Angie Nixon (D)**, and **Neil Gillespie (NPA)**. (Note: Nixon won the Aug. 18, 2026 Democratic primary over Alex Vindman — an outcome not reflected in the earlier research file, which predates that primary.)

**Labeling key:** **[VERIFIED FACT]** = confirmed via a primary source (candidate's own website, official Senate press release, or a direct quote in independent news coverage). **[GAP]** = searched in good faith, nothing found; stated explicitly rather than inferred from party or opponents' framing.

**Research date:** Aug. 26, 2026.

---

## Summary

All three candidates have staked out real, sourced positions on immigration enforcement, and it is the sharpest three-way contrast in the race: Moody has authored and championed federal legislation to expedite deportation of criminal aliens and empower states to enforce immigration law, framing this as "cleaning up Biden's mess"; Nixon has gone further than generic Democratic reform rhetoric, campaigning explicitly to **abolish ICE** and rebuild immigration enforcement "from scratch" under civilian oversight, while comparing ICE agents to "modern-day slave catchers"; Gillespie has said nothing on the topic in any location this research could find. On healthcare, Moody's Senate record includes a December 2025 vote against extending enhanced ACA premium subsidies, but no direct quote from her substantively defending that vote on policy grounds could be located — her floor statements from the same period focus on blaming Democrats for the government shutdown, not on the subsidies themselves; she has, separately, pushed for DOJ recovery of fraudulently obtained ACA subsidy payments, a fraud-enforcement angle rather than an access/affordability one. Nixon has a fully developed healthcare platform (day-one legislation to reinstate ACA subsidies, ultimately "Medicare for All"). Gillespie has not addressed healthcare. On cost of living, Moody has authored specific, narrow bills (flood-insurance tax relief, a law-enforcement homeownership program, staged-accident insurance fraud) but no overarching "affordability" campaign message was found; Nixon has the most extensive platform of the three, with detailed proposals on rent stabilization, property insurance reform, and corporate price-gouging; Gillespie's own questionnaire response addresses only his motivation, experience, and a "separation of powers"/First Amendment priority — cost of living is not mentioned. Gillespie remains, by a wide margin, the thinnest-documented candidate in the field; this file states that gap explicitly rather than guessing at his views.

## ASHLEY MOODY (R) — incumbent

### 1. Immigration enforcement — supports aggressive enforcement

**[VERIFIED FACT]** Moody has authored or co-authored multiple bills to expand and expedite immigration enforcement and deportation, and has framed this explicitly as supporting Trump administration priorities:

- **Expedited Removal of Criminal Aliens Act** (introduced; press release dated **May 21, 2025**): "Following four years of systematic dismantlement of our country's immigration and national security structure under Joe Biden, the American people gave President Trump a mandate to clean up Biden's mess." And: "Today we are finally putting common sense immigration policies first by introducing legislation to authorize the expedited removal of dangerous criminals and prevent activist-judges from keeping known terrorists, criminals and gang members on American soil and endangering our communities." And, criticizing opponents: "Democrats have fought against the quick removal of illegal aliens who have committed atrocious crimes against children or even those that have been convicted of murder."
  Source: Senator Moody press release, May 21, 2025 — https://www.moody.senate.gov/press-releases/senator-ashley-moody-leads-efforts-to-codify-president-trumps-immigration-priorities-introduces-expedited-removal-of-criminal-aliens-act/

- **REMOVE Act** (Rapid Expulsion of Migrant Offenders who Violate and Evade), co-sponsored with Sens. Marsha Blackburn and Ted Budd — would conclude removal proceedings for illegal aliens within 15 days of commencement, "empowering the Trump administration to expedite deportations."
  Source: search-result summary corroborated by Center for Immigration Studies coverage — https://cis.org/Arthur/Fla-Governor-Send-Border-Hawk-Ashley-Moody-Senate . **[GAP]** The primary press release for this specific bill was not independently opened in this pass; the CIS characterization should be checked against Moody's own site before being quoted verbatim.

- Also introduced legislation described as empowering **states** to enforce immigration law and targeting child trafficking at the border.
  Source: Senator Moody press release — https://www.moody.senate.gov/press-releases/senator-ashley-moody-introduces-legislation-to-empower-states-to-enforce-illegal-immigration-laws-prevent-human-trafficking-of-children-at-the-border/ . **[GAP]** Not independently opened/quoted in this pass; title captured via search only.

- **[VERIFIED FACT] Fact-check flag:** PolitiFact rated **False** a Moody claim that the Biden administration "stop[ped] deporting criminals" — the Biden administration deported roughly 158,665 people with criminal charges or convictions in its final two years. This does not negate her enforcement position, but it means one of her stated justifications for it has been found inaccurate by an independent fact-checker.
  Source: PolitiFact, "Ashley Moody is wrong. Biden didn't stop deporting criminals," Mar. 5, 2026 — https://politifact.com/factchecks/2026/mar/05/ashley-moody/immigration-biden-deportation-criminals-policy/

**Bottom line:** Moody's own words and legislative record place her squarely on the "aggressive enforcement" side — expedited removal, shorter deportation timelines, state-level enforcement authority — consistent with her AG-era record (already documented in `us-senate.md`) of suing the Biden administration over immigration policy.

Rick Scott. Florida has roughly 4.7 million ACA marketplace enrollees relying on these tax credits — more than any other state.
Sources: Florida Politics, "Ashley Moody, Rick Scott vote to kill extension of health care subsidies" (paywalled to automated fetch — https://floridapolitics.com/archives/769482-ashley-moody-rick-scott-vote-to-kill-extension-of-health-care-subsidies/ ); syndicated at Miami Times Online — https://www.miamitimesonline.com/lifestyles/health_wellness/ashley-moody-rick-scott-vote-to-kill-extension-of-health-care-subsidies/article_53d2c675-710e-4b6f-8d06-f047b30204e8.html (fetch attempts returned 429/403 in this pass and should be retried directly)

### 3. Cost of living / consumer protection

**[VERIFIED FACT]** Moody has a series of narrow, specific bills rather than a single "affordability" campaign message:

- **Flood insurance:** "As I travel around the Sunshine State, one thing folks continue to tell me is that they are worried about the rising cost of flood insurance." Introduced (with Sen. Rick Scott) the **Flood Insurance Relief Act** to create a tax deduction for flood insurance premiums. Press release dated **July 17, 2025**.
  Source: https://www.moody.senate.gov/press-releases/sens-ashley-moody-and-rick-scott-introduce-bill-to-provide-tax-relief-for-skyrocketing-flood-insurance-costs/

- **Homeownership (law enforcement/first responders):** **HELPER Act** — "The HELPER Act is the next great step in ensuring these heroes know we appreciate their hard work and sacrifices. It will also help them purchase a home in the community where they serve." And: "I am proud that my first bill in the U.S. Senate will help them make their dream of homeownership a reality." Press release dated **March 12, 2025**; described elsewhere as her first bill introduced in the Senate.
  Source: https://www.moody.senate.gov/press-releases/senator-moody-fights-for-affordable-homeownership-for-florida-law-enforcement-officers/

- **Insurance-fraud/consumer-protection framing:** Staged Accident Fraud Prevention Act (targeting staged-crash insurance fraud that "drive[s] up insurance costs for carriers, businesses, and consumers"); a gift-card fraud penalty bill (S.5229, introduced Aug. 4, 2026, already noted in the main research file) — both consistent with her AG-era consumer-protection focus, aimed at fraud rather than affordability or corporate practices directly.
  Source: https://www.moody.senate.gov/press-releases/senator-moody-introduces-staged-accident-fraud-prevention-act-to-protect-all-drivers-on-our-roads-and-prevent-con-artists-from-driving-up-the-cost-of-insurance/

**[GAP]** No broader Moody campaign statement framing "cost of living" or "affordability" as a top-line 2026 message (of the kind Nixon and Vindman have made central) was located. Her documented positions are real but narrower and more piecemeal than her Democratic opponents'.

## ANGIE NIXON (D) — Florida state representative, Jacksonville

All quotes below are drawn from Nixon's own campaign website (angienixon.com/priorities/, retrieved Aug. 26, 2026 — the page carries no visible dateline, so treat it as her current, standing platform rather than a dated statement) unless otherwise noted, cross-checked against independent interview coverage.

### 1. Immigration enforcement — favors abolition/reform, opposes current enforcement model

**[VERIFIED FACT]** Nixon's campaign site has a dedicated platform section, "Support Human Dignity, Ending Mass Incarceration and Mass Deportation," which states in full: *"The US has the largest policing, incarceration and deportation system in the world. Millions of people – disproportionately Black, brown, and poor – are locked in cages or living in fear of a knock on the door, while corporations get rich off their suffering. This is not about safety. It is about control and profit. I don't believe any person is illegal, and I don't believe human beings should be treated as commodities in a system built to punish rather than to heal. Real safety comes from good jobs, strong schools, affordable housing, and healthcare for all – not from more cameras, more cages, and more agents tearing families apart. It is time we had the courage to say it plainly: mass incarceration and mass deportation are moral failures, and I will fight in the United States Senate to end them."*

Specific platform planks under that heading:
- **"A humane immigration system, including a swift path to citizenship for Dreamers and undocumented residents, and a moratorium on deportations pending a full audit of enforcement practices."**
- **"Abolishing ICE and rebuilding immigration enforcement from scratch."** Full text: *"Since its creation in 2003, ICE has operated with almost no accountability – and that's escalated into real tragedy, including U.S. citizens killed by federal immigration agents during enforcement surges in cities like Minneapolis. Angie believes an agency that has repeatedly shown it cannot be trusted with the power it has should not be reformed at the margins – it should be dismantled, with any legitimate immigration enforcement function rebuilt under new, accountable civilian oversight, due process protections, and a mission that reflects American values instead of terror tactics."*
- **"Ending for-profit prisons and detention centers."**
Source: https://angienixon.com/priorities/ (retrieved via direct page fetch, Aug. 26, 2026)

**[VERIFIED FACT] Sharper campaign-trail rhetoric, independently reported:** Nixon has called ICE agents "modern-day slave catchers," a "weaponized paramilitary force," and referred to agents as "THUGS" and "kidnappers"; described detention facilities as a "modern day concentration camp"; and said "We don't need ICE," calling abolition "a moral choice." She also said, regarding the risk of confrontation with agents, "I am concerned that some ICE officials might be shot," and on racial-profiling concerns: "They are going to racially profile us, and they are going to escalate things and they are going to shoot and kill us." Of Republican critics she said, "They are literally trying to kill us, and they are doing it on camera." These remarks were made during campaign appearances and circulated in part via a Republican National Committee-distributed interview clip.
Source: Washington Times, "Angie Nixon compares ICE agents to 'modern-day slave catchers,'" Aug. 22, 2026 — https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/aug/22/angie-nixon-compares-ice-agents-modern-day-slave-catchers/ ; see also Breitbart, Aug. 19 and Aug. 21, 2026 — https://www.breitbart.com/politics/2026/08/19/floridas-socialist-senate-candidate-angie-nixon-campaigns-on-abolishing-ice-granting-swift-amnesty/ and https://www.breitbart.com/politics/2026/08/21/fl-dsa-candidate-angie-nixon-compares-ice-agents-to-modern-day-slave-catchers-calls-enforcement-state-sanctioned-violence/
**Note on framing:** these are two conservative-leaning outlets; the quotes themselves, however, are directly attributed to Nixon and consistent with her own campaign-site language, so they are treated here as [VERIFIED FACT] as quotes, while flagging the outlets' editorial framing (e.g., "socialist," "amnesty") as their characterization, not a neutral description.

**[VERIFIED FACT] Direct action:** On **Aug. 3, 2026**, Nixon joined immigration advocates pressing the Miami City Commission to terminate its **287(g) agreement** with ICE (a local-federal immigration enforcement partnership). The article did not include a direct quote from Nixon from that specific event.
Source: WLRN, "US Senate candidate Angie Nixon joins immigration advocates in pressing Miami to end ICE agreement," Aug. 3, 2026 — https://www.wlrn.org/immigration/2026-08-03/us-senate-candidate-angie-nixon-joins-immigration-advocates-in-pressing-miami-to-end-ice-agreement

**Bottom line:** Nixon has one of the most explicit and repeated documented immigration positions in the race: abolish ICE, rebuild enforcement under civilian oversight, moratorium on deportations pending an audit, and a swift citizenship path for Dreamers/undocumented residents.

### 2. Healthcare / ACA

**[VERIFIED FACT] Campaign website ("Medicare for All" section), full context:** *"The United States is the richest country on earth but doesn't guarantee healthcare to all its people. Americans pay more for worse outcomes than anywhere else in the developed world. That's not a healthcare system, it's a protection racket for insurance and drug companies."* Platform planks include: **"Medicare for All that covers every person in America – free at the point of service, with no premiums, no deductibles, no copays, and no surprise bills"**; comprehensive coverage including dental, vision, hearing, mental health, reproductive/maternity care, and prescription drugs; capping prescription drug costs; expanding home/community-based long-term care; and **"Canceling existing medical debt, wiping it off credit reports for good, and banning debt collectors from coming after patients for medical bills"** (she cites support for Sen. Sanders' Medical Debt Cancellation Act); and paid family/medical leave for every worker.
Source: https://angienixon.com/priorities/

**[VERIFIED FACT] Interview quotes, near-term/incremental framing:** In a **July 8, 2026** interview, Nixon described a path that "starts with extending ACA subsidies, demanding health insurance companies drive down premiums, and reforming the ACA," framing healthcare inflation as a result of corporate consolidation.
Source: FOX 13 Tampa Bay, "One-on-One: Senate candidate Angie Nixon outlines positions on housing, healthcare, and foreign policy," July 8, 2026 — https://www.fox13news.com/news/one-on-one-senate-candidate-angie-nixon-outlines-positions-housing-healthcare-foreign-policy

**[VERIFIED FACT] Democracy Now! interview, Aug. 24, 2026 — direct quotes:** *"Folks are bankrupt simply because they are trying to stay alive. Medical debt is the leading cause of bankruptcy."* *"On day one, I'm going to file legislation to reextend the ACA subsidies."* *"I'm talking to too many people who have loved ones that have died because they were underinsured."*
Source: Democracy Now!, "'Unbought & Unbossed': Angie Nixon on Winning Democratic Senate Race in Florida, DSA Membership & More," Aug. 24, 2026 — https://www.democracynow.org/2026/8/24/angie_nixon

**Bottom line:** Nixon's stated sequencing is a **day-one bill to reinstate expired/expiring ACA premium subsidies** as an immediate step, with **Medicare for All** as her longer-term goal — both directly quoted and consistent across her website and two independent interviews.

### 3. Cost of living / consumer protection

**[VERIFIED FACT] Campaign website, opening platform statement ("A Government that Works for its People, Not Corporations"):** *"Ask any Florida family what's on their mind – whether they're a Democrat, Republican, or don't care much for either party – and you'll hear the same thing: everything costs too much, and their paycheck can't keep up. Since the pandemic, household expenses have jumped nearly a third. In Florida, the cost of living is climbing almost five times faster than it did a decade ago. Meanwhile, wages have stayed the same. Today, only a third of Floridians think our own state is affordable."* And: *"While the average American is struggling, corporate landlords, insurance companies, and grocery chains have posted record profits."*
Source: https://angienixon.com/priorities/

**[VERIFIED FACT] Housing section ("Housing You Can Actually Afford"), full context:** *"Rent is too high, homeownership is out of reach for millions, and Wall Street landlords are buying up housing stock and squeezing renters to enrich themselves. Florida is getting hit the hardest, and is now the worst state in the country for renters. On top of this, Florida homeowners now pay some of the highest insurance premiums in the nation – often as high as $10,000 a year – nearly three to four times the national average."* Specific planks: **"A national rent stabilization standard, just-cause eviction protections, and the right to counsel in eviction proceedings"**; **"Cracking down on corporate landlords and Wallstreet speculators buying up single-family homes and driving up rents"**; **"Federal action on the property insurance crisis, including reinsurance backstops and stronger consumer protections, so Floridians aren't priced out of their own homes by an insurance market that keeps failing them"**; expanding Section 8 vouchers; disaster-resilience funding to harden homes against hurricanes.
Source: https://angienixon.com/priorities/
*(Note: the site's macro-level opening section calls for a "national rent freeze and moratorium on evictions," while the detailed housing section below it calls for "a national rent stabilization standard" — both appear on the same page; quote both rather than picking one, since they are not identical asks.)*

**[VERIFIED FACT] Consumer protection / corporate price-gouging plank:** **"Cracking down on corporate price gouging in groceries, housing, and health care, where record corporate profits have tracked closely with what families are paying at the register."**
Source: https://angienixon.com/priorities/

**[VERIFIED FACT] Interview quotes, Aug. 24, 2026 (Democracy Now!):** *"People who rent, they're paying over 18% [above] the national average. People who have property insurance...are paying 300 times more than folks in other states."* (Note: this specific "300 times" figure is Nixon's own claim, as quoted — it has not been independently fact-checked in this research pass and should be verified before republishing as a statistic rather than a quote.) *"Some parents are paying $800 to $2,400 a month simply to try to send their children to a childcare facility."*
Source: https://www.democracynow.org/2026/8/24/angie_nixon

**[VERIFIED FACT] Earlier interview quote (July 8, 2026, FOX 13):** proposed "federal legislation banning corporations from inflating prices on groceries and fuel," with "heavy fines, penalties, and potential jail time for corporate executives" who exploit consumers; said ensuring "billionaires and corporations pay their 'fair share'" is central to her platform.
Source: https://www.fox13news.com/news/one-on-one-senate-candidate-angie-nixon-outlines-positions-housing-healthcare-foreign-policy

**Bottom line:** Nixon has the most detailed, specific cost-of-living platform of the three general-election candidates — rent stabilization, a federal property-insurance backstop, and corporate price-gouging enforcement, all traceable to her own website and two separate interviews with matching substance.

## NEIL GILLESPIE (NPA) — Ocala

**[GAP — stated explicitly, per the task's instruction to say so rather than guess]** A good-faith search — his campaign blog (neilgillespie4senate.blogspot.com), his one located candidate questionnaire response, news searches, and local candidate-forum pages — found **no stated position from Gillespie on immigration enforcement, healthcare/the ACA, or cost of living/housing/insurance/consumer protection.**

What was actually found and checked:

- **[VERIFIED FACT]** Gillespie completed the **2026 Daytona Regional Chamber of Commerce voter guide candidate questionnaire**, which he posted a link to on his own blog. The questionnaire's substantive content, as retrieved, covers only: his motivation for running, his professional experience, and his top priorities if elected — which he stated as restoring "integrity in our government through the separation of powers doctrine" and protecting First Amendment rights ("freedom of speech and the press, separation of church and state, freedom of peaceful assembly"). **The questionnaire does not contain answers on immigration, healthcare, or cost of living.**
  Source: Daytona Regional Chamber of Commerce questionnaire PDF, linked from Gillespie's blog — https://www.scribd.com/document/1065890247/DRCC-Voter-Guide-Candidate-Questionnaire-PDF ; blog post linking to it — https://neilgillespie4senate.blogspot.com/
- **[VERIFIED FACT]** His blog's other content is overwhelmingly about his personal litigation history (a foreclosure case that reached the U.S. Supreme Court on a cert petition, a wiretapping-law dispute, election-law procedural questions) rather than campaign policy positions. A keyword search of the full blog homepage HTML for "immigration," "housing," "insurance," and "cost of living" returned **zero matches**; "healthcare" appeared only in a post title ("Healthcare in the USA and the World") that was not a 2026 campaign statement.
- **[VERIFIED FACT]** He did **not respond** to the News4JAX 2026 Voter Guide candidate questionnaire (already noted in the main research file).
- **[VERIFIED FACT]** No mainstream Florida news outlet profile, interview, or debate/forum quote addressing any of the three requested topics was located for Gillespie in this pass.

**This should be reported in the voter guide as a documented absence, not an inferred position.** Nothing about Gillespie's likely views should be guessed from his NPA status, his "capitalist, entrepreneur" self-description, or any other candidate's or party's framing.
