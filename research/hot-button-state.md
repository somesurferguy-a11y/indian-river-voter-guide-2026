# CANDIDATE — Robert "Robbie" Brackett (R), FL House 34, incumbent

Method: pulled the actual House roll-call PDFs from flsenate.gov for the marquee 2023/2026 hot-button bills and read them directly (not news summaries). Brackett has been in the House since Nov. 2022, so 2023 was his first regular session and the session where FL passed its 6-week abortion ban, permitless carry, and the "Don't Say Gay" expansion — all three came to floor votes while he was a sitting member.

## 1. Abortion / reproductive rights — FOUND (voting record)
**[VERIFIED FACT]** Brackett voted **YEA** on **SB 300 (2023)**, the "Pregnancy and Parenting Support Act" — Florida's ban on abortion after 6 weeks gestation (with limited exceptions). House final passage vote: **4/13/2023, 4:35 PM, 70 Yeas–40 Nays–9 Not Voting.** Roll call read directly: "Y Brackett-34."
- Source (bill history): https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2023/300 (accessed 2026-08-27)
- Source (roll call PDF, read directly): https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2023/300/Vote/HouseVote_s00300e1107.PDF
- Note: the House companion, HB 7, was never brought to a floor vote — the House "substituted" SB 300 and passed that bill instead, so SB 300's House vote is the correct, and only, record of how Brackett voted on the 6-week ban.
- No 2026-cycle campaign statement on abortion was located on his campaign materials — this vote is the only documented record.

## 2. Guns / Second Amendment — FOUND (voting record)

## 5. Drug policy — PARTIAL FIND (sponsored bill) + GAP on legalization
**[VERIFIED FACT]** Brackett was the **primary sponsor of HB 801 (2026), "Sale of THC-infused Beverages."** The bill would have authorized Florida's Division of Alcoholic Beverages & Tobacco to issue special retail licenses for THC-infused beverages and prohibited sale without one — i.e., a regulate-and-license approach to the hemp-derived-THC drink market (not a ban, not a legalization measure). It **died in the Industries & Professional Activities Subcommittee on 3/13/2026** without a floor vote. A companion, S 1678 (Sen. Truenow), died the same day in the Senate's Regulated Industries Committee.
- Source (sponsor list, confirmed directly): https://www.flhouse.gov/Sections/Representatives/sponsoredbills.aspx?MemberId=4870&SessionId=113 (accessed 2026-08-27) — lists Brackett as First Named Sponsor
- Source (bill history/summary): https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2026/801 (accessed 2026-08-27) — filed 12/17/2025, referred to three committees, died 3/13/2026
- **[ANALYSIS, not fact]** Sponsoring a licensing/regulatory framework for THC beverages signals he sees a legal market to be managed, not eliminated — but this is a narrow, commerce-regulatory bill about a specific beverage category, not a statement on marijuana legalization broadly.

## 6. AI data centers — FOUND (voting record)
**[VERIFIED FACT]** Brackett voted **YEA** on **CS/CS/SB 484 (2026)**, Florida's data-center regulation bill. House final passage vote: **3/11/2026, 4:15 PM, 92 Yeas–16 Nays–9 Not Voting.** Roll call read directly: "Y Brackett-34."
- Source (bill history/summary): https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2026/484 (accessed 2026-08-27)
- Source (roll call PDF, read directly): https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2026/484/Vote/HouseVote_s00484e1809.PDF
- **What the bill does [VERIFIED FACT, from the bill history summary]:** preserves local government authority over comprehensive planning and land-development regulation for "large load customers" (data centers), requires utilities to meet minimum service standards for such facilities, and restricts water management districts from issuing certain permits tied to large-scale data centers — i.e., a bill aimed at protecting local control and ratepayers/water resources from data-center growth, not one that blocks data centers outright. It passed with broad bipartisan support (92–16).
- This gives Brackett a concrete, sourced record on the AI data-center issue, distinct from a campaign-trail statement.
