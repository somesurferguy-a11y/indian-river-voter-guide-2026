# 2026 Primary Election Results — FL House District 34 & U.S. House District 9

**Election date:** August 18, 2026. **Research/compilation date:** August 25, 2026.
**Certification status: NOT YET CERTIFIED as of this writing.** Ballotpedia's results tables for both races carry the standing note "Vote totals may change until results are certified." Florida's canvassing boards certify county results within days of the primary, with statewide certification following; treat every number below as **unofficial** until this file is rechecked against results.elections.myflorida.com (which, as of Aug. 25, 2026, still has no 2026 primary data loaded — see status note below) or Ballotpedia is rechecked for a "certified" label.

## ONE-LINE SUMMARIES

- **FL House District 34 (R primary):** WINNER — **Robert "Robbie" Brackett** (incumbent) defeated Paul Sohi, **20,443 votes (85.7%) to 3,398 votes (14.3%)**, unofficial, district-wide total 23,841 votes (Ballotpedia, aggregating AP/county data).
- **U.S. House District 9 (R primary, 7-way):** WINNER — **Dan Green** (Vero Beach) narrowly led a crowded field, **14,748 votes (25.4%)**, edging out Ben Butler (14,161, 24.4%), Thomas Chalifoux Jr. (11,802, 20.3%), Jorge Martinez (11,324, 19.5%), Justin Story (2,484, 4.3%), Marcus Carter (2,248, 3.9%), and Steve (Howard Steven) Rance (1,335, 2.3%) — unofficial, district-wide total 58,102 votes. Darren Soto (D, incumbent) was confirmed unopposed in the Democratic primary and advances automatically.

This was caught and corrected by checking the page's embedded S3 image path (`vr-enr/OKE/wf_banner.jpg`) and the plausibility of the vote volume (11 precincts, ~3,750 total votes for the CD9 race fits small, rural Okeechobee County, not populous Okaloosa County). **Any future automated/AI-summarized fetch of election data should be spot-checked against a second source before being treated as fact** — this is exactly the kind of silent, plausible-sounding error the no-fabrication policy is meant to catch.
