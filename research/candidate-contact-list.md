## Notes on method / caveats

- Where a campaign's official 2026 candidate-qualifying paperwork (filed with the City of Vero Beach Clerk) was posted online, that was used as the most current and authoritative source, since it is filled out and signed by the candidate directly.
- Where only older filings (2020, 2022, 2024) were found, this is noted — the contact may be stale.
- Facebook profile matches for common names (e.g., "Nicole Paradiso," "Craig MacCoy," "Javi Murillo," "Jose Zamarripa") were NOT relied upon unless the page was clearly and explicitly a campaign page — generic personal profiles cannot be confirmed as the correct person without login access.
- Government office emails/phones are noted as such; they belong to a public office, not a private campaign, but are appropriate fallback contacts for incumbents per the task instructions.
