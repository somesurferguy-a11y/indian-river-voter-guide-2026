### The school-board money pattern
**Matthews (D1), Lange (D2), and Barenborg (D4) draw from one nearly identical roster of retired Vero Beach donors** — the Dyer/Dryer, Barefoot, Atchison, Oglethorpe, Oglesby, Becker, Hunter, Watson, Kingsbury, Schreiber, Mulvoy, Solomon, Schwerin, Flanigan and Trooboff households, most giving the $1,000 maximum to two or three of the three candidates. That is a coordinated donor slate operating across all three school board races, funded almost entirely by individual retirees rather than committees.

**Rosario draws from a different source entirely:** the county Republican Party ($2,500) and two Tallahassee political committees ($2,000 combined), plus a much larger and more small-dollar local base (101 donors vs. Lange's 42). Rosario is the only school board candidate with party or committee money; the other three have none.

**Cross-over is minimal but real:** Warren Schwerin gave to Lange ($500) and to Loar ($1,000, listing "Real Estate"); Linda Teetz gave to Barenborg, Rosario, and Robinson; Marion Oglesby and Ray Oglethorpe gave to McPartlan as well as to the school-board slate. Michael Levin gave $500 to Lange and $500 to Hardingham (D).
