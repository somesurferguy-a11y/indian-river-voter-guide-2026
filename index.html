August 18 primary results

Unofficial results as reported by county and state sources, pending certification. Every figure below links to its source — see the full race-by-race breakdown for details.

Decided outright — no Nov 3 contest for these offices

  • School Board District 2: Jackie Rosario re-elected, 51.84% over Tom Lange
  • School Board District 1: Ryan Matthews — unopposed
  • School Board District 4: Teri Barenborg — unopposed

Advancing to the Nov 3 general election

How this guide works

Every claim has a source

Positions, records, votes, and money all carry a link to where the information came from, with a date. If we couldn't verify something, the guide says so rather than filling the gap.

Facts and allegations are labeled differently

A documented vote is not the same as something a political opponent claimed. The guide tags each item as Documented, Vote in office, Allegation — not established, or Unverified, and every allegation carries the candidate's response.

No endorsements, no ranking

This guide does not tell you who to vote for. Candidates appear in ballot order, every candidate in a race gets the same sections, and third-party endorsements are reported as facts about who endorsed — never adopted.
