## THE ERROR IN FULL (lines 626-642)
The plan recruits "one of Bartholomew the younger, the only patrilineal line out of
Bellwether Creek" (631-632). Trace the roster: Bellwether Creek's sons are Bartholomew the
younger and Hosea (1815-1816, died in infancy); Bartholomew the younger's only son is Josiah
Cray (b. 1840); Nehemiah Cray (b. 1885) is adopted and carries no Cray Y. So the entire
testable Bellwether Creek patriline runs through Josiah — and section 11 (716-718) with the
roster's Purnell entry (45-46) establishes that Josiah's biological father was a Purnell. The
recruit carries Purnell Y-DNA. The stated support condition, "Levin matching Bellwether Creek
and not Slaughter Neck supports the hypothesis" (634-635), is unsatisfiable even if the
hypothesis is true. The "Watch for" line (639-641) half-sees this and hedges it into
harmlessness — "a mismatch on that line MAY be testing his descent rather than Levin's" —
when by the file's own account the line is KNOWN to be compromised. Supports and Falsifies
were left unamended.

Compounding: the outcome space is incomplete. The roster says kinship between the two
Bartholomews "is not proven" (29), not that it is excluded. Two men of one surname in one
Sussex hundred born two years apart are quite likely to share a patriline; if they do,
Levin's descendant matches BOTH comparison lines and the test resolves nothing. The plan
enumerates three outcomes and omits the fourth and most probable — in a section closing with
"A plan that no result could falsify is not a plan" (644).
