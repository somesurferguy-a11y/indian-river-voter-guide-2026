## BIGGEST GAP
Three FamilySearch `Online` templates delete the record's repository and put `citing` in
front of the DGS images the researcher actually examined.

WHY: it is the round-1 fix misapplied in the revision's OWN newest content, in three of the
highest-traffic US templates, so a reader pasting them produces the exact defect section 8
condemns while believing they followed the file. Correcting it forces the writer back through
the layer model, which is the same pass that closes the missing online source-list entries
and the `citing`/LCCN misuse — three findings for one piece of work.
