# Research report template

Copy this file, rename it `reports/YYYY-MM-DD_[client-or-self]_[surname]_report-[n].md`, delete
the guidance in `> quote blocks`, and fill it in. Rationale and section-by-section explanation:
`references/documentation-standards.md` §5.

The same skeleton serves a client report and a report to your future self. The audience changes
the register, not the sections. Your future self has forgotten everything and cannot ask you a
question, which makes them harder to serve than a client, not easier.

A research report records an **episode of research**. It is not a compiled genealogy: it is
organised by the process, it shows the failures, and it is superseded by the next report. See
"Report vs. compiled genealogy" in §5 of the reference file for the distinction.

## 4. Sources searched

> **Every** source consulted, productive and not. This section is how "reasonably exhaustive"
> becomes checkable, how you avoid re-searching the same records in 2031, and what makes any
> later argument from absence available to you. For a client it is also most of what they paid
> for: eight hours that eliminated four record groups is real work, and a report that hides it
> looks like eight hours that found nothing.
>

## 9. Notes on privacy, consent, and restrictions

> Include whenever the research touched living people, DNA, sensitive findings, or material under
> repository or licence restriction. Record what is being withheld from this report and why, what
> consents exist, and what the recipient may and may not republish. Protocol:
> `references/documentation-standards.md` §9. For client work, sensitive findings are told to the
> client privately **before** the written report goes out, not discovered by them in it.
